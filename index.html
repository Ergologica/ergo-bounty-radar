The Radar score (0–100) ranks bounties by how promising they look for a contributor today. Four transparent parts:

• Value (up to 40) — ERG-equivalent reward, log-scaled so mid-size bounties still register.
• Maintainer attention (up to 25) — recently touched issues are far more likely to get reviewed and paid.
• Low competition (up to 20) — fewer comments, fewer people circling.
• Accessibility (up to 15) — starter-friendly flag and stack. Set a 🎯 profile and the languages you know take over this part.

It's a heuristic to aid discovery, not advice — always read the issue before reserving.
